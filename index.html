02 Company & approach

From a problem
to a prototype.

We build ventures around problems we can investigate, model and test.

For SmartPark, that means learning from drivers and operators, representing parking facilities down to individual bays, and developing the software to connect them.

What the prototype supports ↗
01 / Understand

Start with the people.

Customer discovery with drivers, operators and parking stakeholders helps define the problem and the workflows to explore.

02 / Build

Make the idea testable.

Spatial models and software turn a facility layout into a prototype where bookings, allocations and bay states can be explored.

03 / Validate

Work towards deployment.

Test architectural decisions and assess integration requirements before taking the product into an operating environment.
