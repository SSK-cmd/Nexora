01 Product workflows

One facility.
Two perspectives.

Parking information is often split across layouts, bookings and access rules. SmartPark brings these together around the individual bay.

01 / Operator

See and manage the parking environment.

Work from a spatial model of the facility, using 2D and 3D views to understand the spaces you manage.

  1. Review the facility and individual bays
  2. Update bay states manually
  3. Apply access and allocation rules
  4. Review booking-derived availability
02 / Driver

Move from discovery to arrival with clarity.

Connect the choice of a suitable space with a booking and guidance towards the allocated bay.

  1. Discover a parking location
  2. View suitable space options
  3. Select and confirm a booking
  4. Navigate towards the allocated bay

04 Development status

What works today.
What comes next.

SmartPark is under active development and validation. The prototype supports the core workflows above; deployment depends on further testing and integration work.

Prototype today

Modelled spaces and bookings.

The prototype demonstrates spatial parking models, 2D/3D views and booking and allocation workflows for operators and drivers.

  • Bay states are managed manually.
  • Availability is derived from bookings, rather than live occupancy sensors.
  • The visuals on this site use illustrative data.
Under assessment

Connections to live operations.

Further work covers the integration architecture and the systems needed for an operating facility.

  • Live sensor and occupancy feeds
  • Gate, access-control and payment systems
  • External platform links and deployment with operating partners
