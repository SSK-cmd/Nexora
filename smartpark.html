SmartPark in motion

A clearer journey,
imagined.

From a city of possibilities to a space that fits. A short vision of more connected parking.

01 The opportunity

Parking information is often fragmented.

Drivers need clearer information about where they can park, whether a space suits their needs and what happens after they arrive.

Operators need a more complete view of individual spaces, bookings, rules and changing operational requirements.

02 One platform

Two connected
experiences.

01 / Operator

See and manage the parking environment.

Turn spatial layouts into an operational view of individual bays, bookings, rules and availability.

  1. Explore 2D and 3D representations
  2. Manage individual bay states
  3. Apply access and allocation rules
  4. Review booking-derived availability
02 / Driver

Move from discovery to arrival with clarity.

Help drivers identify a suitable parking location, book and receive guidance towards an allocated bay.

  1. Discover a parking location
  2. View suitable space options
  3. Select and confirm a booking
  4. Navigate towards the allocated bay

04 Designed for interoperability

Built to connect,
not create another island.

We are assessing SmartPark against APDS 4.1 and the UK National Parking Platform technical architecture to understand how it can complement existing parking infrastructure.

This is active research and architecture work—not a claim of current certification or production integration.
